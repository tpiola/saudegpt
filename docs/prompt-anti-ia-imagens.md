## Regras para Todas as Gerações
- ❌ NUNCA usar: "perfect, flawless, hyperrealistic, octane, unreal engine, cinematic lighting, masterpiece"
- ✅ SEMPRE adicionar: "real photograph, documentary style, authentic Brazilian everyday life, natural skin and imperfections"
- ✅ Textos: "perfect legible Brazilian Portuguese, correct medical terms, no invented words, sharp typography"
- ✅ Pós-geração: upscale no Magnific com "increase photorealism, natural skin texture, remove any digital/AI look, real Canon photo, subtle grain"
